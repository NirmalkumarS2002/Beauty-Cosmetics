Common Questions

Frequently Asked

A few things our customers often ask before reaching out to the team directly.

helpDo I need to book an appointment in advance?

Yes, we recommend booking at least two days ahead for facials and bridal services, since studio slots fill quickly on weekends.

helpCan I reschedule or cancel my booking?

You can reschedule up to 12 hours before your appointment at no charge through the link in your confirmation email.

helpDo you offer home service appointments?

Select services like bridal makeup and hair styling are available at home within city limits for an additional visit fee.

helpHow can I track my product order?

Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS, or you can reach our support team with your order number.
