About Us

We Believe Beauty
Starts With Care

Beauty Cosmetics began as a small studio with one goal: to make premium skincare and makeup that people could trust, without the noise of a thousand ingredients they couldn't pronounce.

Today we work with dermatologists and formulators across the world to bring that same promise to every jar, tube and bottle we ship.

Our Journey

The Story So Far

From a single skincare formula to a full beauty house, here is how we got here.

2018

A Small Studio Begins

Two formulators started mixing skincare in a rented studio, selling their first serum to friends and neighbours.

2020

Our First Collection

The Radiance skincare line launched, selling out within three weeks of release.

2022

Makeup Enters The Studio

We introduced a full makeup range, formulated to be as gentle as our skincare.

2026

A Beauty House Today

Now shipping across the country, with a growing family of skincare, makeup, hair and fragrance.
